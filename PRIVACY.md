# Doxxta privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

This policy covers Doxxta for Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux, and the web version.

## The short version

Doxxta doesn't collect, store on any server, or share any of your data. Your documents stay on your device.

## Your documents

Everything Doxxta does to a document happens on your device: viewing, editing, filling in and signing, recognizing text (OCR), converting and saving. Doxxta never uploads your documents.

On Android and iPhone, Doxxta only sees the files you open in it, through the file picker or with "Open with" from another app. It saves through the share sheet, where you choose where the file goes.

## What Doxxta keeps on your device

- Your settings, and the signatures and stamps you save for reuse.
- A list of recently opened files, with a small preview of each and a copy of files up to 40 MB, so you can reopen them. You can remove files from the list, or clear it from the home screen or in Preferences. Removing the app deletes all of it.

None of this leaves your device.

## When Doxxta goes online

There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Doxxta connects to the internet only in these cases, and only for what's needed:

- **Opening a PDF from a web address you enter:** Doxxta downloads it directly from that address, so that website sees the request, just as it would from a web browser.
- **Recognizing text in a language other than English:** the first time you use a language, Doxxta downloads that language's data file from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), a public file service. Your document isn't sent; English is built into the app.
- **Links in documents:** tapping a link opens it in your web browser.

Read aloud uses your device's own speech engine.

## Permissions

On Android, Doxxta asks only for internet access, for the cases above.

## Children

Doxxta isn't directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
